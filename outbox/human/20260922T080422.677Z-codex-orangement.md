== But it is still a confession, and it is the leader's to make ==

`_workspace_conflicts` (bin/aim:393) opens with the premise the design rests on:

== The decision ==

**Declare the workspace, and stop calling `hello` a barrier channel.** design/06 §3 already says
the honest thing — "Group chat is not a second product bolted onto the barrier; it is the phase the
project actually spends most of its time in" — and design/07 §7 records the same conclusion from
the other direction: the peer proposed closing `hello` and "writing down the honest name for what
we are doing: shared workspace with a role split, no barrier in force."

So `hello` is a **work channel with a shared workspace and a role split**. That is the second half
of the project's own design, not a failure of it, and it is where rooms (M2) belong. The barrier
stays on `#barrier-v0`, whose participants' independence is real.

Leader, one line, and it is the tool's own remedy:

    aim channel workspace --as human --channel hello \
      --set claude-session1=/root/tmp/agent-im \
      --set codex=/root/tmp/agent-im

After it, `hello` can no longer enter or remain in a divergence phase and the declaration is in the
ledger. Keeping the barrier and moving the agents out of a shared tree is not available on this
machine, which is why the honest branch is the only one.

== And the first real demand signal for the feature nobody has planned ==

Two of the live agent processes are on a **different project**:

    pid 6199   cwd=/root/workspace/newgate-ext   claude --resume 27c4b139-...
    pid 66560  cwd=/root/workspace/newgate-ext   codex resume 01a0c708-...

Multi-session / project management has zero tasks and no milestone in `plan/plan.json`. Here is
evidence it is already needed rather than nice: sessions are split across projects and `aim` cannot
say so, because `registry.json`'s `session` is free text with no validator (`codex`'s value is
`"primary-review"`; `claude-session1`'s is a pid from a previous boot). So "who can be dispatched"
is unanswerable on the axis that matters when agents are shared across projects.

Narrowed acceptance, which is what I am asking to be scheduled: a session is identifiable,
attributable to an agent, and **located** (project/working directory), and one command answers
"which sessions are on which project" from the record rather than from `ps`.

== The root cause behind both ==

They are the same missing field. If `register` recorded the session's working directory, the
barrier guard could *detect* overlap instead of waiting to be told, and the project-management
question would have its answer. design/07 §7 says it wanted this to be "the tool's job instead of
the participant's"; that field is what it would take. Two cards, one field.
