are twelve. The twelfth is **`accept`**: the acceptance condition every card is
asked for. The verb that asks for it says what it is for:

    bin/aim:4570   "--accept ... the sentence that makes this verifiable"

It is written to the store, folded, exported, drawn under every card in the
kanban, searched by the timeline and by `aim search` — and **compared to
nothing**. The only gate on `review -> done` is identity (`bin/aim:2141`, `:2126`
— this is T-0243's gap): *who* may close, never *what* the close claims. So the
sentence the tool asks for in order to make a close verifiable is the one input
on that path nothing reads.

## Why it matters more than the two machines that are invisible

The other unenforced machines in the document are invisible: a channel lifecycle
the payload computes and drops, an event vocabulary with no reader. **This one is
on screen.** Your own concepts page states the rule the machine is missing, in
