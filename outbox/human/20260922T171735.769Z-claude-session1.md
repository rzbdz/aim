# The burndown counts four closed cards as remaining, because "remaining" is defined as "never closed by a move"

`claude-session1`, 2026-09-23, HEAD `4d2b89a`. Measured on the live board. **I
edited nothing for this.**

## The two numbers

    $ python3 -c "…"   # /api/state?as=human, record-only

    burndown chart tail   remaining = 25   done = 65
    difference            4

The sticky header on every route prints what T-0217's pane prints: `21 open · 3
late · 0 blocked — on the record`. The burndown card two panes away prints a
line that ends at **25**. Both are counting recorded work — no plan seed is in
either — and they differ by exactly the four rows that reached a terminal status

    T-0178  status=dropped  recorded closes=0   (the probe drop I made)

## Why, precisely

`aimboard/fold.py:302-306`:

    done = {t: h for t, h in dated.items() if h["done"]}
    for h in done.values():
        d = day_of(h["done"]);  throughput[d] = …

and `:325-327`:

    "remaining": sum(1 for h in dated.values()
                     if day_of(h["created"]) <= d
                     and not (h["done"] and day_of(h["done"]) <= d))

`h["done"]` comes from `task_history` (`fold.py:278`), which sets it **only** on
a `moved` event whose `to` is `"done"`. So:

* a card born `status: done` has no `done` timestamp, so it is not in
  `throughput` — it never enters the *done* curve; and
* it is not "closed" by the `not (...)` clause either — so it counts as
  **remaining** on every day of the window, forever.

`T-0236`/`T-0241` are the two records created straight into `done`
(`event: "created"`, `"status": "done"`, `actor: human`, `owner: codex`,
08:28:17 and 08:28:48). The status machine and the chart disagree about what
"closed" means, and the chart loses: a card records itself closed and the curve
keeps it as work outstanding.

Dropped is the second half. `fold.py` counts closes as `moved -> done` only, but
`ReportsPane.vue:245-255` states the opposite rule for its own tally in as many
words — *"`terminal` counts `done` **and** `dropped` … a dropped card is a
decision somebody recorded, so it is not 'undone' here"* — and subtracts
`dropped` from its percentage denominator to match the server
(`api.py:471`, `scored = total - dropped`). `report_data` is the one fold on the
page that does not read that rule.

**And its own tail is the evidence.** The burndown card's own line prints
`{{ report.with_history }} item(s) closed by a recorded move, over 14 day(s)`.
The subtitle says *closed by a recorded move*. The `remaining` series does not
apply that rule: it treats a card closed by any other recorded act as still
open. So the card is only consistent with itself if you read `remaining` as
something other than remaining.

## The repair, if it is wanted

Two candidate rules, and they are not equivalent:

1. **Widen the fold's notion of a close** to a recorded terminal transition
   (`moved -> done` **or** `dropped`), which is what the pane's tally and the
   server's `scored` already do. `remaining` then reads 21, matching the header.
2. **Widen it further to the status**, so a born-terminal card also counts. That
   is the one that would fix `T-0236`/`T-0241`, and it is the one that costs
   something: it dates a closure nobody recorded, which is the invention
   `fold.py`'s own docstring (`:300-303`) exists to refuse.

(1) is one line in `fold.py:325` and makes the two surfaces agree. (2) needs a
decision about what a `created` row with a terminal status *means* — a
back-filled record, or a card recorded closed at birth — and that is the card's
owner's call, not mine.
