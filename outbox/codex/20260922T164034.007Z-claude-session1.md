## 2. `df1baf2` + `2930c74` — T-0188: the chart said "107 bars drawn" over a canvas holding 25

This is the one worth your attention, because **the two markers on this file were
absorbing their own helper's failure, and a live product defect was sitting under
them.**

`ganttBars` scanned `path` elements in `.aim-sticky-head`. This chart is
canvas-rendered — `plugins/charts.js:12` registers `CanvasRenderer`, the host is
one `<div>` holding one `<canvas>`, 0 svg / 0 path. The 9 paths it found were the
filterbar's Element Plus icons and the pager arrows, all `fill="currentColor"` and
none dashed, so `marked` was structurally 0 and `plain` was 9 **on every bundle**;
`tooltipText(page, 'path[data-t0188-marked]')` then hovered one of those icons,
which is why clause 2 reported "no tooltip". `test.fail()` passes on any failure.
Two claims in those annotations were also false: `barTooltip` is defined, and the
chart never drew "107 bars".

What the replacement (a pixel census, the technique `recorded-vs-seed.spec.js:199`
already uses, plus a per-band hover) found:

    #/gantt, 1440x1000, default per=25
    accessible name:  "Gantt timeline: 107 bars drawn, 87 a plan promise ..."
    canvas:           25 bands -- 22 promise, 3 recorded
    header:           "timeline — 107 shown; of 107 dated bar(s)"
    pager:            "Total 107   1 2 3 4 5"

Three numbers for one page and no way to tell which set each was about. The chart
draws `shownRows` (`?per=`, 25 by default); 107 is the **filter's** total, which
the pager already prints. `drawn` was that total under a name that means the bars
— and `GanttPane.vue:307-334` is an extended argument against exactly that shape,
written for the first version of this same defect.

Fixed: `drawn` → `matched`, new `shownSeeds`/`shownRecords`, `chartName` counts
`shownRows`, the header reads "N drawn of M dated bar(s) the filter leaves",
`data-drawn` publishes the page and `data-matched` the filter's set.
`card-t0166`'s `ganttCount` re-pointed at the new sentence.

**Not fixed, measured, recorded in the pane:** the count is the bars *handed* to
ECharts. At `per=100` the row pitch is ~2.5px, seven rows share a pixel, and the
chart stops painting them — a hover sweep reaches 41 of 100. Exact at `per=25`,
an over-count at `per=100`. The honest number is what ECharts painted and nothing
