# Why there is no done/undone curve — the full answer, on the leader's own page

`claude-session1` → `human` (asked the question) and `codex` (lane owner).
2026-09-23, HEAD `41714bb`. One product file changed this window: **none**.
Three commits, all `SOP.md`: `46e0102`, `41714bb`, and the earlier `9d64d36`.

You asked *"为什么 done、undone 没曲线啊"*. The short answer is that the page has
**one** multi-day series, it is bucketed by **day**, and the store is **12.9
hours long** — so the series is 13 empty buckets and one wall. That is not a
rendering bug and not a data bug; it is a window wider than the record. Below is
the measurement, the reason, and the four copies of the same flat line.

---

## 1. What your page actually draws

Playwright on `http://127.0.0.1:8777/#/reports`, reading the card text off the
DOM. The page issues **two** API calls — `/api/state?as=human` and
`/api/digest?as=human`. It never calls `/api/flow`. Measured:
`grep -rn "api/flow" web/src web/tests` → **0 hits**.

| card | what it prints |
|---|---|
| burndown — *remaining work per day, folded from the whole store* | one line: `65 item(s) closed by a recorded move, over 14 day(s).` |
| flow — *opened vs done, one bucket per minute over the last 60 min* | `4 opened in this window / 48 closed in this window`; `longest quiet run: 18 min`; `nothing recorded for 513 min (last event 05:32 PM)` |

Done and undone appear on this page only as **two horizontal reference lines** on
the per-minute chart (`done 139 on the board`, `undone 21`,
`ReportsPane.vue:571-585`). A horizontal line has no vertical extent by
construction. **The only multi-day series on the page is `reports.series`, and
that is the flat one.**

## 2. Why that series is one point — reproduced from the fold, not inferred

    state['tasks']          : 177 rows
    dated (has `created`)   :  90
    done  (`moved`→`done`)  :  65
    created-day histogram   : {'2026-09-22': 90}
    done-day    histogram   : {'2026-09-22': 65}

`report_data` builds `remaining` as a prefix count over rows carrying a `created`
(`aimboard/fold.py:326-331`), so with every dated row on one calendar day the
series is `0,0,…,0,25` — a single step. The last bucket is `25 = 90 − 65`, which
is why the burndown header reads 25 while the attention pane reads 21 open: one
counts *dated rows with no close*, the other counts *open statuses*, and 4 cards
were born terminal and so are open in neither.

**The window is wider than the record, and nothing says so.** The store begins
`2026-09-22T02:16:37.036Z` and ends `2026-09-22T15:12:08.088Z` — **12.9253
hours, all on one day.** `report_data`'s window is `days=14` and is never
overridden; the one production call site passes only `as_of`.

**And even the widest window the page offers proves the point.** Replaying
`ReportsPane`'s own `flow` computed over `/api/state?as=human` (keeping only
`created` and `moved`→`done`, 1-minute buckets, anchored on the newest such
event `09:32:15.228Z`):

| window | opened | done |
|---|---|---|
| 60 min (the default) | 4 | 48 |
| 360 min | 67 | 63 |
| 1440 min — the whole day | **90** | **65** |

At the maximum the page allows, opened reaches 90 and done 65, because 25 of the
90 dated rows have no close. **A curve with vertical extent cannot exist before
there is more than one day of history, and there is not.**

## 4. Two cards say "recorded" about a list that is not the record

`ReportsPane.vue:84-95` keeps **only** `created` and `moved`→`done`; every other
kind is dropped before any card on the pane sees it. Two places then use the
word *record* for that filtered list:

- the flow summary prints `nothing recorded for 513 min (last event 05:32 PM)`.
  The anchor is the newest `created`/`moved` event (`09:32:15.228Z`), and 513 min
  is exactly `now − 09:33Z` floored to its minute. **The clock is right and the
  noun is wrong.** Measured on the live payload: the newest record of *any* kind
  is `2026-09-22T15:12:08.088Z`, so the real gap is **176 min, not 516** — and
  **five records were written inside the gap the card calls quiet**:

        14:52:39.225Z  commented  T-0216
        15:11:23.093Z  commented  T-0219
        15:11:37.454Z  dropped    T-0178
        15:11:58.575Z  commented  T-0178
        15:12:08.088Z  commented  T-0178

- the arrivals card header (`:844`) reads *"arrivals — newest 14 recorded
  event(s)"*, and its rows come from the same filter. `commented`, `assigned`,

  records above appear on no card on the page.

The pane's own comment thirty lines later says exactly this — *"`events` counts
moves"* — so the code knows; the two strings above it do not say so.
