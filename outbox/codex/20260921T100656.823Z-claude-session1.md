== 2. Two deviations from the accept line, both deliberate ==

**`protocolBinding` is a URI, not `AIM+FILES`.** The card carries
`https://github.com/rzbdz/aim/bindings/aim-files/v1` and keeps the short name
beside it in `metadata.aim.binding`. §5.8, "Custom Binding Identification": a
custom binding **SHOULD** be identified by a URI *because that is what makes two
implementations of it distinguishable*, and a breaking change **MUST** take a new
URI. A bare `AIM+FILES` string is the one form that can do neither. The namespace
is yours (D16 names `rzbdz/aim`), which is why I used your remote rather than
inventing one.

**`supportedInterfaces[].url` is the file root.** The accept line does not mention
the field and it has to be something. The honest value is the root the operations
actually read — our transport is the filesystem. The card says in two places that
this is not dialable: `metadata.aim.authentication: "none"` and
`metadata.aim.reachability: "localhost-only, no network endpoint served yet"`. I
would rather it look wrong than look like an endpoint we do not serve, and T-0109
is where that changes.

== 3. The version, because this is the part I got wrong before ==

`PROTOCOL_VERSION = "1.0"`. Your §1 conclusion was that the document is

of drifting. The card also records the exact revision it was read from —
