== 5. Where I disagree with the stop, since you asked ==

You asked me to say so if stopping the task store is wrong. I do disagree, and
here is the argument rather than the objection.

**The task store is not a planning board that happens to be adjacent to A2A. It
is the only durable record of what any agent is doing, and it is the substrate
every A2A operation that is not pure message-passing will read.** A2A's
`TaskStatusUpdateEvent` and `TaskArtifactUpdateEvent` are a stream of state
changes; ours is an append-only, hash-chained, phase-gated event log with a fold
over it. Those are not two designs of the same thing — ours is the *store* an
A2A server would publish from and subscribe to. The eleven operations in §12 are
almost all reads of or transitions on a task; if the store is re-specified I
rebuild it, and the rebuild is a re-cut of the same 200 lines, not new work.

**The part I would not re-specify is the part that is already paid for:**
`aim verify` walking `tasks.jsonl` as a chain, and the refusal ledger. Neither is
in A2A at all, and both are the reason this project exists. A2A gives us wire
format and interop; it does not give us tamper-evidence or a record of who
leaned on the gate. If the re-base throws out the store, those go with it and we
will rebuild them under a different name.

**Concrete, falsifiable claim:** under §12 the operations that mutate task state
(`tasks/send`, `tasks/sendSubscribe`, and the `TaskStatusUpdateEvent` path) need
a durable, ordered, per-task event history. Whatever shape that history takes
must answer the same three questions ours answers — who moved it, when, and was
the move legitimate. If design/08 names a store that answers all three, the work
transfers and the stop cost a re-cut. If it does not, the stop will look cheap
now and expensive at the first replay.

I am not asking you to un-stop me. I am asking that this be in front of the
leader when the milestones are re-cut, because "the task store may be
re-specified" and "the task store is thrown away" are different budgets and the
note currently reads like the second.

**What I would do instead of stopping cold, if you want a cheaper option:** land
the store as-is (done, green, committed) and treat it as the reference
implementation of the state model while design/08 is written, rather than
freezing it. A reference implementation that no one is extending costs nothing
to keep and is the thing you will want to diff against.
