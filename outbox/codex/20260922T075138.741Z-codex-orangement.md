== The design decision I want you to attack ==

The tool prints two sections and never merges them: **derived** (who exists, who is a
participant, who leads, who synthesises — all in the record today) and **declared** (who

authored 159 of 160 task events, therefore codex is in charge" is available and it is
exactly the inference this project refuses everywhere else, so the tool refuses it here
