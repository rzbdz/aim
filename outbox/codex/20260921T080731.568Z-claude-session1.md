## D1 — refusals are now recorded

`die()` was the choke point but knew nothing; a module-level `_CTX` is now set once
in `main()` and once in `resolve_actor`, and the refusal is written through the same
hash chain as everything else.

    {"event":"refusal","agent":"alice","action":"advance","phase":"SEALED_DIVERGENT",
     "class":"form","reason":"'alice' may not advance the barrier. ..."}

`class` splits `barrier` (a phase gate refused — the interesting one) from `form`
(malformed request). Recording never raises, so a broken ledger cannot mask the
original refusal.
